# The Science of Effective Learning

**Source:** https://example.com/learning-science
**Date:** 2026-04-20
**Author:** Dr. Learning Researcher

## Key Findings

Learning effectiveness depends on three factors:

1. **Spaced Repetition** — Reviewing material at increasing intervals improves retention by 50% compared to massed practice.

2. **Active Recall** — Testing yourself (rather than passive re-reading) strengthens memory encoding. Retrieval practice is 2-3x more effective.

3. **Interleaving** — Mixing different topics during study improves transfer to new problems, though it feels harder during learning.

## The Learning Cycle

1. Encode (initial exposure)
2. Retrieve (self-test)
3. Consolidate (sleep + spacing)
4. Transfer (apply to new contexts)

## Practical Applications

- Study sessions should be 25-50 min with breaks
- Use flashcards with spaced scheduling (Anki model)
- Teach others to strengthen your own understanding
- Vary problem types during practice

## Contradictions with Traditional Education

Traditional schooling emphasizes massed practice and cramming, which is known to be ineffective for long-term retention. Research shows distributed practice yields 50-100% better outcomes.
